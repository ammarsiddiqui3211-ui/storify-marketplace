1. Introduction

Welcome to Storify Marketplace ("Storify", "we", "our", or "us"), available on web at https://shop.storifyco.com and through our official Android application (Package: com.storifyco.shop).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you browse our marketplace, register an account, purchase products, or sell items as a verified vendor.

Our Promise: We never sell your personal data to third parties. We collect only what is strictly necessary to process your transactions, coordinate deliveries, protect buyer and seller escrow, and secure your account.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on whether you use Storify as a buyer, a visitor, or an authorized seller, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, phone number, and encrypted password authentication via Supabase Auth.
  • Shipping & Delivery Details: Recipient name, street address, city, province/state, postal code, and delivery notes.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, transaction totals, reference IDs, payment method selected (such as Cash on Delivery, Escrow, Bank Transfer, or mobile wallets).
  • Seller Application & Verification Data: Store name, business location, identity/CNIC verification details, bank account/Easypaisa/JazzCash details for payouts, and product catalog media.
  • Communications: Customer inquiries, dispute resolution messages, and support feedback.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Connection Information: Device model, operating system version, browser type, IP address, and general network geolocation.
  • Usage Analytics: Pages visited, products searched, and interaction metrics used exclusively for site reliability and performance optimization via privacy-respecting analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business and transactional operations:

  • To facilitate seamless browsing, product search, cart checkout, and order fulfillment.
  • To operate our buyer and seller escrow protection system, ensuring fair payments and valid dispute resolution.
  • To notify you about order confirmations, tracking numbers, and seller portal status via automated email and push alerts.
  • To verify seller credentials and prevent fraudulent listings, unauthorized payments, or abuse.
  • To maintain performance, resolve bugs, and enforce our marketplace Terms of Service.

4. Device & App Permissions (Android)

When using our mobile application on Android devices, we may request certain system permissions:

  • Camera / Photo Library: Used only when you choose to take or upload product photos, store logos, or buyer dispute evidence. Never accessed in the background without user initiation.
  • Storage / Media: Required to cache web assets for fast offline performance and save downloaded receipts.
  • Push Notifications: Used with your consent to notify you regarding order status, shipment tracking, and important security alerts.

5. Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not share your information with third-party advertisers. Information is disclosed solely in the following operational scenarios:

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Your shipping address and contact number are provided to trusted delivery partners exclusively to deliver your orders.
  • Verified Vendors: Sellers receive order details and destination addresses necessary to pack and dispatch purchased items.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: We use industry-standard enterprise cloud platforms (Supabase for encrypted database storage and authentication; Vercel for hosting delivery; Resend for transactional emails).
  • Legal & Fraud Protection: Where required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the safety, escrow integrity, and property of Storify Marketplace users.

6. Data Security & Escrow Protection

We employ multi-layer security measures to keep your data safe:

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All traffic between your device and Storify servers is encrypted via TLS 1.3 / SSL. Database records are protected by strong database encryption.
  • Strict Row-Level Security (RLS): Our database policies strictly restrict database reads and writes so users can only access their authorized records.
  • No Plaintext Passwords: Account passwords are cryptographic hashes managed securely by Supabase Authentication.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize browser cookies and localStorage strictly for functional purposes:

  • Maintaining your active login session and authentication tokens.
  • Saving cart items and localized currency or category preferences.
  • Enabling the Service Worker offline cache for smooth PWA and mobile performance.

You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies; however, some marketplace features (like maintaining an active shopping cart or seller dashboard) may require them.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to comply with statutory accounting and tax regulations.

Your Rights: You have the right to request access to your stored personal data, request corrections, or request complete account and data deletion at any time by emailing us at storify.store.co@gmail.com. Upon verification, your account and associated personal data will be promptly removed from our active systems.

9. Children's Privacy

Storify Marketplace is intended for general audiences aged 18 and older (or minors with parental supervision). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
